# Weekly Product Pulse: Feb 16-22, 2026
_Week: 2026-02-16 - 2026-02-22_

This week's feedback highlights user appreciation for app simplicity while revealing significant concerns. Critical issues include real-time data bugs leading to losses and high brokerage fees. Users also requested new features like paper trading, indicating a desire for expanded functionality.

## Themes
- **Glitches & Bugs** - Users reported significant dissatisfaction due to critical bugs affecting real-time data, calculations, and display, which led to trading losses and hindered usability.
- **Fees & Charges** - Customers expressed strong negative sentiment regarding high brokerage and F&O charges, often finding them disproportionate to earnings and lacking transparency.
- **Unclassified** - General feedback showed positive sentiment for simplicity but also indicated a desire for new features like paper trading, alongside some unelaborated dissatisfaction.

## Quotes
- "After completing a trade, the balance does not update immediately. Because of this delay and incorrect balance display, I have faced losses."
- "In total earnings of ₹90, I ended up paying ₹350 as charges."
- "The mobile app is very simple and user friendly."

## Actions
- Prioritize investigation and resolution of real-time data and calculation inaccuracies to prevent user losses and improve trading.
- Review current fee structures and enhance transparency around all charges, especially for F&O and 'extra' costs.
- Evaluate demand and feasibility for frequently requested features such as paper trading and advanced charting options.
